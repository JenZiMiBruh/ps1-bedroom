## Description

> **Leave it on. Fall asleep to it. It's 2001 forever.**
>
> PS1 Bedroom is a little late-night bedroom that looks like a PlayStation game: wobbly low-poly 3D, dithered colour, chunky textures and real light rays through the blinds.
>
> The room follows **your real clock and your city's weather**. The sun and moon cross the sky on real paths, it rains when it rains outside, and every sound is placed in 3D: rain on the glass, the CRT hum, the ceiling fan, crickets, a train going by.
>
> **Pick what's outside your window.** 15 places, each with its own sounds, climate and temperature: Downtown, a Skyscraper 40 floors up, Neon City, a Neighborhood, the Suburbs, your Backyard, a Trailer Park, Arizona, a Desert Canyon, a Tropical Island, a Beach Boardwalk, Lakeside, Forest, Mountains and a Countryside Farm.
>
> **Things to do:**
> - Play games on the PlayStation (every GTA, as tributes)
> - Put your own albums on the boombox, cover art and all
> - Watch YouTube on the CRT
> - Use a 2001 PC: messenger, Minesweeper, dial-up
> - Pet the German Shepherd
> - Answer the phone, pop in a VHS tape
> - Set a sleep timer and drift off
>
> **Android:** background playback, lock-screen controls, home-screen widgets, and it looks great on a TV with Samsung DeX.
>
> Free, no ads, no account. Your music never leaves your device.
