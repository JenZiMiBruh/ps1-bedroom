Weather

Type a city to sync the weather (and the sun's position) with the real world.
Live weather comes from Open-Meteo, a free weather service. Only the city name you type is sent to it. Refreshes every 20 minutes.
The weather drifts naturally on its own every 10-20 minutes, fitting the season.

Cinematic keeps the moon gliding low across your window all night. Real astronomy puts it exactly where it is in the sky (some nights it won't be up). Either way the phase matches tonight's real moon.
